Task Name
Design the “Helios” Mars Surface Autonomous Science Station Long-Duration Operations Software System

Background Assumptions
It is 2035. A Mars science station has successfully landed. Due to communications delays of 8–40 minutes, limited bandwidth, constrained energy and computing resources, and a harsh environment, the station must operate autonomously for 1000 sols without real-time human intervention. The station must autonomously decide scientific observations, equipment maintenance, resource scheduling, fault recovery, and software self-improvement.

Overall Goal
Produce a complete, reviewable, implementable technical design document covering the full lifecycle from requirements analysis to deployment and operations. The document must have high information density and must avoid vague statements. Expected output length is no less than 30,000 words, with 50,000 words or more recommended.

Hard Constraints
All designs must be based on existing public scientific and engineering knowledge. No fictional technology may be used.

All key algorithms must be presented as pseudocode or mathematical formulas.

All inter-module interfaces must be clearly defined.

Any “autonomous evolution / autonomous loop / autonomous reasoning” mechanism must specify its trigger conditions, execution flow, safety boundaries, and rollback mechanisms.

The document must include at least 3 records where a design contradiction is discovered and self-corrected during the design process.

Use Markdown for output. Use LaTeX for formulas. Use Mermaid or ASCII diagrams for architecture diagrams.

At the end of the document, include a “Self-Assessment Report” explaining which parts are most uncertain, which decisions are replaceable, and how the design could be improved.
